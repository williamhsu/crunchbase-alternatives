# Crunchbase Alternatives (2026)

A plain comparison of tools people use instead of Crunchbase to find startups, funding rounds and the people behind them. Free options first, then paid.

## Free alternatives

| Source | What you get | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| [SEC EDGAR Form D](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) | Official US filings: amount raised, executives, filing date. Companies file within 15 days of the first sale. | US only, raw data, no industry search, no contact details |
| [Crunchbase free account](https://www.crunchbase.com) | Company profiles and recent rounds | Limited searches, no exports |
| [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co) | Free public company and ecosystem profiles | Strongest in Europe; filters need a paid plan |
| [Y Combinator directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies) | Every YC company with batch, industry and status | YC companies only |
| [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com) | Startup profiles and open roles | Built for hiring, not research |
| [VCBacked free plan](https://www.vcbacked.co/recently-funded-companies) | 5 verified funded-startup leads a week with founder contacts | Full database and exports are paid |
| [Apollo.io free tier](https://www.apollo.io) | B2B contact data and email credits | Limited funding details |

## Paid alternatives

| Tool | Best for | Pricing (public or typical) |
|---|---|---|
| [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com) | Institutional investors: fund, deal and valuation data | Custom, typically five figures a year |
| [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com) | Corporate strategy and market research | Custom, typically five figures a year |
| [Harmonic](https://harmonic.ai) | Early-stage VC deal sourcing | Custom |
| [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com) | Sector and theme tracking, international coverage | Paid plans from roughly $1,500/year |
| [Dealroom (paid)](https://dealroom.co) | European ecosystem intelligence | Custom |
| [VCBacked](https://www.vcbacked.co) | Outbound sales to recently funded startups: founder emails, unlimited CSV exports | $79/month or $299/year |
| [Apollo.io](https://www.apollo.io) | General B2B prospecting and sequences | From $49/month |
| [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com) (for reference) | Broad company research | Pro about $588/year, Business about $2,388/year |

Prices change; check each vendor before buying. Last reviewed October 2026.

## Which one to pick

- **Selling to newly funded startups:** you need fresh rounds plus contacts. VCBacked, or Apollo paired with a funding source.
- **Investing or doing diligence:** PitchBook for depth, Harmonic for early-stage sourcing, Dealroom for Europe.
- **Market and trend research:** CB Insights or Tracxn.
- **Occasional lookups on a $0 budget:** SEC EDGAR plus Crunchbase's free account.
